# PF App Critique: Framing

> **Prompt:** You'll be critiquing Slack. It's intentional! We want to see how you respond to a product that shares some of the same challenges we think about at PermitFlow: surfacing the most urgent and important items, coordinating inputs from multiple users, and bringing structure to messy, complex communication.
>
> Brainstorm a few focused areas for improvement, then choose one to explore in depth. Share your point of view on:
>
> * What isn’t working: Identify the core problem and where the experience falls short.
> * Impact on users and the business: Explain how it affects user experience, task success, confidence, or efficiency, as well as relevant business outcomes.
> * Why this problem: Briefly share the other problems you considered and why you chose to focus on this one.

## 1. Purpose

- **Product under review:** Slack (desktop app).
- **Why this product:** it shares PermitFlow's core challenges: surfacing the most urgent and important items, coordinating inputs from multiple users, and structuring messy, complex communication.
- **User need (Helen):** Slack is about interactions between people and holds the context for what happened in a project. People working together, especially new people, need to reference what someone else worked on or find a person with experience in a domain, and need an easy way to connect with them with the right context. Today that context is hard to find up front: you have to dig and search for it.
- **Company need:** show a point of view on these shared challenges that transfers to PermitFlow.
- **Session goal:** brainstorm a few focused improvement areas, choose one, and explain what isn't working, its impact on users and the business, and why this problem over the others.

### Brainstormed problem areas (Helen)

1. **Fragmented search.** Search bars in the sidebar, in each major tab, in the directory, and at the top of the product (meant to search everything).
2. **What needs attention is hard to organize.** A lot of information; few ways to organize or customize how it's viewed. Custom views exist but are limited in scope.
3. **Thin profile page (leaning).** Finding context on a person means searching and setting up filters; a richer profile page could put that context up front.
