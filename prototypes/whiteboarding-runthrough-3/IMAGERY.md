# Imagery: Saltmoor

## Style brief

Saltmoor is a $1,200 natural hybrid mattress for social shoppers who care about sleeping cool, back support and clean materials. The page uses warm off-white, sand and a muted sea blue, set in Geist with a calm, plain-spoken tone. The images should feel like the same place: a calm, warm-white bedroom in soft morning daylight, with natural oak and linen. They are editorial home-goods photographs, not glossy ads. There are no people, text or logos, and the mattress is always fully visible.

## Prompt template

Photorealistic product photograph of a premium hybrid mattress on a low natural oak platform bed, {angle}. The mattress is about 12 inches thick with a smooth white organic cotton cover, a subtle quilted top panel, and a soft knit side border in warm off-white. {styling}. Calm minimalist bedroom with warm white plaster walls, pale sand-colored linen curtains, soft morning daylight from a window on the left, gentle shadows. Muted palette of warm white, sand, oak and a hint of soft sea blue-grey in the light. Clean, airy, natural, editorial home-goods photography, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no people.

## Settings

- Format: JPEG, 2K
