## Open decisions

- **Landing hero CTA alignment.** "Start publishing" sits left-aligned under the headline, which conflicts with the right-align rule. I recommend keeping it left, because a hero is read top to bottom and the button continues the headline and lede. Everywhere else (page headers, footers, sheet footers, cards) actions are right-aligned.
- **Radio and checkbox borders.** The unselected radio and checkbox borders are light. If they ever feel too faint in testing, raise it as a library decision rather than overriding them in a prototype.
- **Long titles in the submitted banner.** The banner repeats the full title, which runs two lines for a 100-character title. I recommend keeping the full title, since it's the confirmation of which book was submitted. If it feels heavy, cut it at about 60 characters with an ellipsis.

## Checked, left as is

- "How review works" uses a small muted 16px icon in front of each line. The text carries the information and the icons don't compete with it, so I left them.
- The first-book offer box and the sheet callouts lead with their heading text; the icon only supports it.

## Held up

- Actions are right-aligned with the primary on the far right in page headers, sheet footers and the flow footer. The flow footer and sheet footer are fixed below a full-width divider.
- Status tabs with counts sit directly above the table, in workflow order with All first. Table cells are structured: badges, dates, review type, and right-aligned tabular-number prices, sales and royalties. There are no sentences in cells.
- Four font sizes in all (12, 14, 16, 24), with weight and muted color for the finer steps.
- You can always see where you are: the active store nav item has a flush underline, breadcrumbs appear below the top level, the sheet has a backdrop over its page, and the flow shows its name, "Step N of 4" and an exit.
- Empty state (no books yet) explains what goes there and offers the action. Validation errors appear inline next to the field.
- Confetti only runs when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion (`prefersReducedMotion()` returns no pieces), and the sheet and spinner drop their animation under reduced motion.
- Every control is a semantic button, input or label. Focus is visible (package ring tokens plus a 2px outline on the prototype's own buttons). The icon-only bell, search and close buttons have accessible names.

- During the pass someone else was clicking in the preview pane, so I checked the sheet's scroll lock in code rather than by scrolling.
